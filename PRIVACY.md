# Pal Again Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Pal Again is an independent, open-source iOS and iPadOS client. It contains no advertising SDK and no first-party analytics or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

- Your Muse Gadget SDK Token and pairing credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.
- Chat history and selected preferences are stored on the device.
- If you enable iCloud chat sync, chat text is stored in your private iCloud key-value store under your Apple ID.
- Photos stay on the device where they were selected or captured unless you explicitly send them to Muse.

You can clear local chat history, disable synchronization, revoke permissions, replace your SDK Token or remove the saved Muse pairing from the app's Settings screen.

## Data sent when you use Muse

Pal Again sends data only when it is needed for a feature you choose or a Muse command you approve. Depending on what you use, this can include:

- chat messages and Muse responses;
- photos or camera captures you choose to send;
- voice recordings you choose to send;
- device information requested by supported commands, such as location, motion, battery state or local-network results.

This information is sent to Muse services through the authenticated gadget connection. Muse's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

## Permissions

Bluetooth and Local Network access support pairing and discovery. Camera, microphone, speech recognition, location and motion access are requested separately and can be denied or revoked in iOS Settings. Pal Again continues to expose only the features allowed by the permissions you grant.

## Local network and Shortcuts

Pal Again asks before permanently allowing a Muse-requested Apple Shortcut or a local-network target. Approved items can be removed later in Settings.

## Contact and deletion requests

Pal Again has no developer-operated account database. For questions, bug reports or privacy requests, open an issue in the [public repository](https://github.com/exrector/muse-gadget-ios/issues). Do not include SDK Tokens, pairing credentials, private messages, photos or other secrets in an issue.

# Политика конфиденциальности Pal Again

Обновлено: 4 ноября 2026 года

Pal Again — независимый клиент с открытым исходным кодом для iOS и iPadOS. В приложении нет рекламы, собственной аналитики и отслеживания пользователей.

SDK Token и данные пары хранятся в iOS Keychain. История чата и настройки остаются на устройстве; при включённой синхронизации текст чата хранится в вашем личном iCloud. Фото, голос, сообщения и запрошенные данные устройства передаются Muse только при использовании соответствующей функции. Разрешения можно отозвать в настройках iOS, а историю, синхронизацию и пару — удалить в настройках Pal Again.

Вопросы и запросы можно оставить в [Issues репозитория](https://github.com/exrector/muse-gadget-ios/issues), не публикуя токены, данные пары, личные сообщения, фотографии и другие секреты.
