Pal Again turns an iPhone or iPad Apple no longer updates into a gadget for Muse, Meta's AI assistant: chat, photos, voice notes, and live voice and video calls with Muse's talking avatar. Built on Meta's open Muse Gadget SDK, open source.

## Maker's first comment

Hi Product Hunt! Meta opened the Muse Gadget SDK so anyone can build hardware for its Muse assistant, with clients for ESP32 boards and Linux. I ported its protocol to iOS so an iPhone or iPad you already own can be that gadget, down to an iPhone 6s on iOS 15.

What it does today: a chat with Muse by text, photo or voice note; Muse's own avatar and name; widgets; device features Muse can use, such as the camera, speech, sensors and Apple Shortcuts; and in 2.0, live voice and video calls, where Muse's avatar talks in sync with its voice. Each gadget gets its own chat in Muse.

Two honest notes: you need a Muse account and an SDK token from gadgets.muse.ai, and calls depend on your account, since Meta turns them on or off per account.

The code is open, and reports from any old device, working or not, are very welcome.
